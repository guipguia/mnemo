# Governance

mnemo is maintainer-led until it has enough contributors to justify more structure.

## Roles

- **Contributor:** anyone who opens an issue or PR.
- **Maintainer:** reviews and merges PRs, cuts releases, triages security reports.
  Listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, quality contributions over ~3 months, nominated by a maintainer,
approved by a majority of current maintainers. Inactive for 6 months → emeritus.

## Decisions

Lazy consensus in issues/PRs. If there is disagreement, maintainers vote; simple majority wins.
Changes to this document need a 2/3 maintainer majority.

## Vendor neutrality

No single company may hold a majority of maintainer seats once there are 3+ maintainers.
